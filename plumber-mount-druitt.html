Built Fast, and Built All At Once

Mount Druitt went up quickly through the 1960s and 70s, and a great deal of it was built to the same specification at the same time. Sixty years on, that shows in the plumbing.

Same materials, same age, same failures, arriving in roughly the same order: earthenware sewer under mature trees, galvanised water pipe at the end of its life, and hot water systems that were replaced once and are now overdue again.

The advantage of a suburb like this is that very little about it is a mystery. We have almost certainly been inside the same house before.

What We Get Called For

Common Plumbing Problems in Mount Druitt

Housing built to one pattern, wearing out to the same pattern sixty years later.

Tree roots in earthenware sewer

Sixty-year-old drainage under trees the same age. Roots get in at the joints and catch everything. Jetted out, then a camera to check whether the pipe is still sound.

Galvanised pipe at the end of its life

Low pressure and discoloured water. The bore narrows from the inside until almost nothing gets through. Replaced in sections or in full, whichever actually makes sense.

Hot water due again

A unit replaced twenty years ago is now as old as the one it replaced. We will give you a straight answer on whether a repair is worth doing.

Blocked kitchen and laundry waste

Decades of fat, grit and detergent narrowing a waste line until it stops. A jetter restores it properly; a supermarket drain cleaner does not.

Taps and toilets

Old tapware and cistern parts that have been dripping or running for years. Quick to repair, and it shows up on the next bill.

Emergency callouts

Burst pipes, gas leaks and sewer overflows, 24 hours a day. Pipework this age does not choose a convenient moment.

FAQs

Plumbing in Mount Druitt - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Mount Druitt?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

The same drain blocks every year. Can that be stopped?

Usually, yes - but not by clearing it again. If it comes back on a schedule there is a crack or an open joint letting roots in, and that section needs relining or replacing to actually end it. A camera inspection shows you the break so you are deciding on evidence rather than guessing.

Is it worth repiping the whole house or just the bad section?

Depends how much of it is galvanised and how bad it is. If one branch is restricted, replacing that run solves it. If the whole house is original and the pressure is poor everywhere, doing it piecemeal usually costs more in the end. We will tell you which situation you are in rather than quoting the bigger job automatically.

Do you come out to Mount Druitt after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

Which council area is this?

Mount Druitt sits in the Blacktown City Council area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
