Older Streets, and a Lot of Building Behind Them

Wentworthville is largely older housing with a steady amount of newer development mixed through it - unit blocks near the station, and a great many duplexes and secondary dwellings behind existing houses.

That second category is where a lot of our work is. Putting a granny flat or a duplex on an existing block means running new drainage, water and gas to a property whose original services were never designed to carry it.

The older houses themselves bring what housing of that age brings: earthenware sewer, corroded water pipe, and hot water that has outlived its second warranty.

Common Plumbing Problems in Wentworthville

Old houses, new dwellings behind them, and the services that have to stretch to cover both.

Granny flats and secondary dwellings

New drainage, water and gas runs to a dwelling at the back of an existing block, connected properly and to code rather than teed off whatever was nearest.

Tree roots in old drainage

Earthenware lines with decades of growth over them. Roots get in at the joints and catch everything. Jetted clear, then inspected with a camera.

Original water pipe under load

An older service feeding more fixtures than it was ever sized for. Low pressure is often the service rather than the tapware, and it is worth checking which before replacing anything.

Units and shared drainage

In the newer blocks, a blockage in shared drainage surfaces well away from its cause. We find where it actually is, and whose it is.

Hot water systems

Repairs and replacements, including second systems for a new dwelling at the rear.

Gas fitting

Cooktops, heaters, hot water and BBQ points, run and leak tested to code, including new runs to a secondary dwelling.

FAQs

Plumbing in Wentworthville - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Wentworthville?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

We are putting a granny flat at the back. What does the plumbing involve?

New drainage to the sewer, a water service sized for the extra fixtures, and gas if you are running it. The part people underestimate is the existing service - the original one was sized for one dwelling, and simply teeing off it often means neither place has decent pressure. Worth getting looked at before the slab goes down, not after.

The pressure dropped after we added a second bathroom. Why?

Usually the incoming service rather than anything in the new work. More fixtures drawing on a pipe sized for fewer will show up as poor pressure everywhere, particularly when two things run at once. It is fixable, and it is a different job from replacing tapware.

Do you come out to Wentworthville after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

Which council area is this?

Wentworthville sits in the Cumberland City Council area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
