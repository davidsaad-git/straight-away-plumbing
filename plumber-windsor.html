A Historic Town on a Flood Plain

Windsor is one of the oldest towns in the country, and the plumbing runs the full span: genuinely old buildings in the centre, postwar housing around them, and semi-rural properties beyond.

The river is the other factor. Properties on the flood plain deal with things most of Sydney never thinks about - water arriving from outside rather than from a pipe, and drainage that has to cope with a great deal more than an average storm.

Out of town, plenty of properties run on tanks and pumps with on-site waste rather than mains and sewer, which is a different job again.

Common Plumbing Problems in Windsor

An old town, a flood plain and a lot of rural property, each with its own demands.

Tanks, pumps and filtration

Rainwater tanks, pressure pumps and filtration on properties that are not on mains. Serviced, repaired and replaced, including pumps that short cycle or have stopped priming.

Stormwater and surface drainage

Drainage that has to move serious volume. Blocked and broken stormwater lines, downpipe connections and water sitting where it should not.

Old drainage in the town

The oldest lines around here predate most of the standards now in place. Roots, cracks and collapses. Jetted clear and inspected with a camera.

Aging pipework

Low pressure and discoloured water on anything that has never been replaced. Renewed in sections or in full.

Hot water systems

Repairs and replacements in town and on rural properties, including systems running off tank water.

Gas fitting

Cooktops, heaters, hot water and BBQ points, installed and leak tested to code - including bottled gas where there is no mains connection.

FAQs

Plumbing in Windsor - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Windsor?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

My property is on tank water. Can you still help?

Yes. Tanks, pressure pumps, filtration and the pipework around them are normal work for us. If the pump is short cycling, the pressure has dropped or the water has changed taste or colour, that is usually the pump or the filtration rather than the tank itself.

Is there anything worth doing before flood season?

Clearing stormwater lines, gutters and downpipes is the obvious one, because they are the things that have to work hardest and they are cheap to check. It is also worth knowing where your main water shut off is and that it actually turns - the middle of an emergency is a poor time to find out it is seized.

Do you come out to Windsor after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

Which council area is this?

Windsor sits in the Hawkesbury City Council area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
