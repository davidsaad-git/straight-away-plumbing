Units, Older Homes and Plenty of Rentals

Westmead is a mix of apartment blocks, older houses and a large institutional precinct, and the plumbing splits along much the same lines.

In the units it is shared stacks and common property. In the older houses it is original drainage and pipework that has been in the ground a very long time.

There is also a lot of rental and investor-owned property here, which usually means the person with the problem is not the person paying for it. We are used to working with agents, and to being the one who explains the job to somebody who is not standing in the room.

FAQs

Plumbing in Westmead - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Westmead?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

I am a tenant. Can I call you directly?

You can call us any time and we will tell you what the problem is. Who authorises and pays for the work is between you, your agent and the owner, and for anything beyond an emergency we will need that sorted before we start. If it is an emergency we will make it safe first.

I manage a rental. Do you deal with tenants directly?

Yes. Give us the tenant's details and we will arrange access with them, then report back to you in writing with what we found and what it cost. It saves a round of messages in both directions.

I am in an apartment. Is a blocked drain my problem or the building's?

It depends where the blockage actually is. Inside your own fixtures and branch lines it is normally yours. In the shared stack or common drainage it is normally the building's. We find out which before anybody commits to paying, and we can put that in writing for your strata manager.

Which council area is this?

Westmead sits in the City of Parramatta area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
