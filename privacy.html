Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you give us, in plain English.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Who we are

Straight Away Plumbing (ABN 63 241 616 098, contractor licence 372223C) is a licensed plumbing business operating across Sydney, New South Wales. This policy covers this website and the quote form on it.

What we collect

The only information we collect directly is what you choose to type into our quote form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you give one
  • Your suburb or address, if you give one
  • The service you need and any message you write

If you phone or email us instead, we have whatever you tell us in that conversation. We do not ask for payment card details through this website, and you should never send them to us by email.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, give you a quote, arrange a time, and carry out the plumbing work. That is the whole purpose. We do not add you to a marketing list, we do not send newsletters, and we do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

How it reaches us

The quote form is handled by Formspree, which passes your submission on to us by email. Nothing you submit is stored on this website itself, and this site has no database, no accounts and no logins.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no tracking cookies and does not follow you across other sites.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It is privacy-focused and cookieless: it reports totals such as page views and which site referred you, and does not identify individual visitors. That is also why you are not being nagged by a cookie banner.

Third parties

A small number of services make this website work:

  • Cloudflare - hosts the website and provides its analytics
  • Formspree - delivers quote form submissions to us
  • Behold - supplies the Instagram photos shown on our home page

Each handles data under its own privacy policy. Some are based overseas, including in the United States, which means information you submit through the form may be processed outside Australia.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job records for as long as we need them to do the work, answer questions afterwards, honour any warranty on our workmanship, and meet our tax and record-keeping obligations. After that they are deleted.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call 0403 322 290 or email straightawayplumbing@gmail.com and we will sort it out. There is no charge, and we will not make it difficult.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmitting information over the internet is ever completely secure, but this website is served over HTTPS, so what you send through the form is encrypted in transit.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first and we will do our best to put it right. If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
