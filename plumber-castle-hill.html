Established Streets and New Apartments

Castle Hill has two distinct kinds of property. The established streets are older homes on generous blocks with gardens that have had decades to grow. Closer in, there is a growing amount of newer apartment and townhouse development.

The older homes bring the classic set: roots in the sewer, aging pipework, hot water units well past their warranty, and original bathrooms finally being pulled out.

The newer buildings bring strata questions instead - shared stacks, common property, and blockages that show up a long way from where they actually are.

Common Plumbing Problems in Castle Hill

Two kinds of building in one suburb, and two separate sets of problems.

Tree roots in the sewer

Big established gardens sitting over old drainage. Roots get into the joints and catch everything that passes. Cleared with a jetter, then a camera to check the condition of the pipe.

Aging pipework

Falling pressure and discoloured water in a house that has never been repiped is usually galvanised steel corroding from the inside. Replaceable in sections or in full.

End-of-life hot water

A storage unit gets ten to fifteen years. Plenty around here are past that. We repair what will hold and replace what will not.

Strata and shared stacks

In the newer buildings, a blockage in the stack shows up in the lowest bathroom on the line. We find where it really is before anybody pays for it.

Bathroom renovations

Original bathrooms coming out, often with pipework behind the tiles that should go at the same time. Full scope, rough-in to fit-off.

Gas fitting

Cooktops, heaters, hot water and BBQ points, installed and leak tested to code, including bringing older runs up to standard.

FAQs

Plumbing in Castle Hill - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Castle Hill?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

I am in an apartment. Is a blocked drain my problem or the building's?

It depends where the blockage actually is. Inside your own fixtures and branch lines it is normally yours. In the shared stack or common drainage it is normally the building's. We find out which before anybody commits to paying, and we can put that in writing for your strata manager.

Can tree roots be removed permanently?

The roots can be cut out completely, but if the pipe is cracked they will find their way back in - the crack is the invitation. Clearing it buys you time; relining or replacing that section is what stops it recurring. A camera inspection tells you which one you are looking at.

Do you come out to Castle Hill after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

Which council area is this?

Castle Hill sits in the The Hills Shire area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
