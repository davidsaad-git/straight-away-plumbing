Common Plumbing Problems in Baulkham Hills

Older housing stock and big established gardens produce a fairly predictable set of jobs.

Tree roots in the sewer

The single most common job out here. Roots find the joints in old earthenware and clay lines, get inside, and catch everything that goes past. We cut them out with a jetter, then put a camera down to see whether the pipe itself is still sound.

Old galvanised pipework

Dropping pressure and rusty looking water usually mean galvanised steel corroding from the inside. Houses of this era that have never been repiped get there eventually. It can be done in sections or in full - we will tell you which is worth it.

Hot water systems at end of life

A storage unit gets ten to fifteen years if it is looked after. Plenty out here are past that. We repair where a repair will actually hold and replace where it will not, rather than charging you twice.

Blocked gutters and stormwater

Mature trees drop a lot into gutters, and what gets past ends up packing the stormwater line. When it backs up the water comes down the side of the house instead. Better cleared before a storm than during one.

Bathroom and kitchen renovations

A lot of original bathrooms around here are being redone, and behind the tiles there is often pipework that should go at the same time. We handle the full scope, rough-in to fit-off, working in with your builder.

Gas fitting

Cooktops, heaters, hot water and BBQ points, installed and leak tested to code. On older homes that often means bringing the run up to standard rather than just connecting the new appliance.

Plumbing in Baulkham Hills - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee to come to Baulkham Hills?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

Why do Baulkham Hills homes get so many blocked drains?

Mature trees and older sewer lines. A lot of Baulkham Hills was built from the 1960s onward on generous blocks, and the trees planted then now have root systems sitting right on top of earthenware and clay sewer pipes. Roots find the joints, get in, and catch everything that passes. Cutting them out clears it, but a CCTV inspection is what tells you whether the pipe itself needs relining.

Do you come out to Baulkham Hills after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

My water pressure has dropped and the water looks rusty. What is it?

Usually old galvanised steel pipework corroding from the inside. It is common in houses of this era that have never been repiped. The pressure drops as the bore narrows and the water picks up colour on the way through. It can be replaced section by section or in full, and we will tell you which makes sense for your place.

Which council area is this?

Baulkham Hills sits in the The Hills Shire area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
