FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer emergency plumbing?

Yes - we're on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Burst pipes, blocked drains, gas leaks, overflowing toilets and no hot water are all things we'll come out for, day or night. There's no call out fee, and getting to you quickly is what keeps the damage, and the repair bill, down.

What plumbing services do you offer?

Everything from a dripping tap to a full renovation rough-in: blocked drains and high pressure jet blasting, hot water system repairs and replacements, burst and leaking pipes, leaking taps and toilets, CCTV drain inspections, drainage and stormwater, gas fitting, water filtration and general maintenance - for homes and businesses alike.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. It matters more than it sounds: unlicensed work can void your home insurance and often has to be pulled out and redone properly later.

What areas do you service?

We cover most of Sydney, 24 hours a day. Search for your suburb on our service areas page, or give us a call and we will tell you straight away whether we can get to you, and when.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Both. Houses, townhouses, strata and apartment buildings, shops, offices and commercial sites - one-off repairs through to ongoing maintenance. On renovations we'll work in with your builder and the other trades so the plumbing lands when it needs to.

What does it cost, and do you charge for quotes?

Quotes are free and there's no call out fee. We'll tell you what the job involves and what it will cost before we start, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.
