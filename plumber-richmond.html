Old Buildings and Rural Properties

Richmond has a historic centre, established housing around it, and rural properties spreading out from there. The plumbing changes completely depending which of the three you are in.

In the older buildings the materials are the issue - drainage and pipework that predates most of the standards now in place, and which nobody has surveyed in a very long time.

On the rural blocks it is self-sufficiency: tanks, pressure pumps, filtration, long pipe runs between buildings, and waste systems that are not connected to anything.

Common Plumbing Problems in Richmond

Town and country in the same postcode, needing quite different work.

Tanks, pumps and filtration

Rainwater tanks, pressure pumps and filtration. Pumps that will not prime, pressure that has dropped away, and water that has changed taste or colour.

Long rural pipe runs

Water and gas running between house, sheds and outbuildings, often installed decades ago by whoever was available. Traced, repaired and brought up to standard.

Old drainage

The oldest lines here are older than most of the rules. Roots, cracks and collapses, cleared with a jetter and assessed properly afterwards.

CCTV inspections

On an old or large property nobody knows where the drainage runs. A camera and a locator answer that without digging a series of holes to find out.

Hot water systems

Repairs and replacements, including systems running off tank water and units a long way from the main house.

Stormwater and yard drainage

Water pooling against buildings after rain, blocked lines and downpipes that are not connected to anything useful.

FAQs

Plumbing in Richmond - Your Questions

Do you charge a call out fee in Richmond?

No. Call out is free and so is the quote, at any hour, anywhere we service. You are told what the job involves and what it will cost before any work starts, so nothing on the invoice comes as a surprise.

My pressure pump keeps switching on and off. What causes that?

Short cycling, and it is usually either a failed pressure vessel, a leak somewhere on the line, or a pump that has lost its prime. It is worth sorting quickly - a pump cycling constantly wears itself out, and if the cause is a leak you are also losing tank water you paid to collect.

We are not connected to the sewer. Do you work on on-site waste systems?

We work on the plumbing side of them - the lines running to the system, and blockages or failures in that pipework. Tell us what the system is when you call and we will tell you straight away whether the job is ours or whether you need the system's own service agent.

Do you come out to Richmond after hours?

Yes, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, at no extra call out charge. Burst pipes, gas leaks, sewer overflows and dead hot water units do not keep business hours.

Which council area is this?

Richmond sits in the Hawkesbury City Council area, and Sydney Water handles the water and sewer mains. For most household jobs it makes no difference, but it matters on anything touching the sewer main or needing approval, and we will tell you upfront if your job is one of those.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is carried out by a licensed plumber, backed by insurance and completed to Australian plumbing standards. Our licence number and ABN are at the bottom of every page on this site.
